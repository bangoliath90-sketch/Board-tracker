Install Board Tracker

Track inventory in grams, who took each bag, and who still owes. Pick your phone below. It takes about two minutes.

Android

Installs as an app

On a computer or another screen? Scan this with your Android camera. On your phone already? Tap the button.

Download for Android
  1. Open the downloaded Board Tracker file.
  2. If Android blocks it, tap Settings, turn on Allow from this source, then go back and tap Install.
  3. If Play Protect warns you, tap More details → Install anyway. It shows this for any app that isn't from the Play Store.

iPhone

Runs in Expo Go

After installing Expo Go, point your iPhone Camera at this code and tap the banner that appears.

Get Expo Go
exp://u.expo.dev/973c30f2-6ea6-494d-8a0d-f4e5e53ecf07?channel-name=preview
  1. Install Expo Go from the App Store.
  2. Scan the code with your Camera, or copy the link above, paste it into Notes and tap it.
  3. Board Tracker opens inside Expo Go. Next time, open Expo Go and pick it from your recent projects.

After you install

  1. Open Board Tracker and tap Create account.
  2. Tap Join with code and enter your name.
  3. Enter the invite code from the person who invited you. It looks like PEG-XXXXXX.

You only see a board after entering its invite code. The app updates itself, so there's nothing to reinstall when new features arrive.

Forgot your password? Tap Forgot password? on the sign-in screen and open the email's link on the same phone.
